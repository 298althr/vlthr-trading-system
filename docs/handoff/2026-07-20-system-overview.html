Business Logic — How We Make Money

The system trades crypto perpetual futures on Bybit. The thesis is simple: use a multi-factor signal scoring system (DQS) to identify high-probability entries, size positions using Kelly criterion bands, and let the edge compound over many trades.

Signal Pipeline

  • Data ingestion fetches 15m OHLCV + funding + OI for 5 symbols: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB
  • Adaptive scorer computes a DQS (Data Quality Score) 0-100 per symbol per bar, weighted across technical (40%), structure (30%), context (30%)
  • DQS tiers: Fair (50-64), Good (65-74), Excellent (75-84). DQS 85+ is vetoed (Kelly=0, negative edge detected in calibration)
  • Kelly bands scale risk: DQS 80 → 2.5x, 75 → 1.5x, 70 → 1.0x, 65 → 0.7x, <65 → 0.3x. Mean-reversion strategies get 3x boost
  • Risk per trade: base risk_pct from tier (fair=2%, good=3%, excellent=5%) × Kelly multiplier. So a DQS 70 trend-following signal = 2% × 1.0 = 2% risk
  • Position sizing: dollar_risk = balance × (risk_pct/100), qty = dollar_risk / SL_distance. Capped at balance × 4x leverage
  • Portfolio gates: Risk Budget Ledger (12% max portfolio risk), Trade Count Guard (max concurrent trades), Correlation Guard (no correlated positions)

Revenue Model

With a $50k Bybit account and 2-5% risk per trade, each trade risks $1,000-$2,500. If the strategy achieves a 35%+ win rate with R:R ≥ 1.5 (the backtest target), expected value per trade is positive. At 4-8 trades per week, the system targets 2-4% monthly returns. The edge comes from DQS filtering — only trading when the multi-factor score says probability is favorable — and Kelly sizing — betting bigger when edge is stronger.

Dual Execution

Every approved signal fires on both paper (Postgres simulation, $10k virtual) and Bybit demo ($50k real USDT). Paper trades validate the strategy logic. Bybit trades validate execution, slippage, and fees. The only intended difference is account balance — risk_pct is the same. This lets us compare simulated vs real-world performance.

What Happens When a Signal Fires

  • Pipeline scores symbol → DQS computed → passes all gates → signal approved
  • Paper trade created in Postgres with PENDING status
  • Bybit wallet balance fetched → position sized using same risk_pct × Kelly
  • Market order placed on Bybit with TP/SL attached
  • bybit_order_id stored in paper_trades row to link them
  • Backend monitor (15s interval) watches both paper and Bybit for SL/TP/time exit
  • On close: PnL recorded, risk ledger updated, Telegram notification sent

Known Constraints

  • Min notional: $5 per trade (Bybit requirement)
  • Qty step: Symbol-specific (BTC=0.001, SOL=0.1, XRP=1)
  • Max leverage: 4x (self-imposed, Bybit allows 10x+)
  • Session filter: Only trades during London, NY open, NY late sessions
  • DQS veto: DQS ≥ 85 = no trade (negative Kelly edge)

What We Haven't Looked Into

This is the most important section. The system has significant gaps before going live on Bybit mainnet.

Strategy & Performance

  • Win rate is 20% — backtest target was 35%+. The DQS scoring or SL/TP placement may be miscalibrated for current market conditions. No investigation has been done into why live performance diverges from backtest.
  • SL placement too tight — 32 SL hits vs 4 TP hits suggests SL is too close to entry. The ATR-based SL multiplier (2.0-2.5x) may need widening, or the entry timing is poor.
  • Kelly bands unvalidated — The V7 Kelly bands (0.3x to 2.5x) were calibrated on historical data. No forward-testing has confirmed they produce positive EV in live conditions.
  • DQS 85+ veto — The system vetoes trades at DQS 85+ (Kelly=0, "negative edge"). This means the highest-quality signals are never traded. The rationale and calibration behind this needs validation.
  • No regime adaptation — The system classifies regimes (TREND/MIXED/MR) but the regime gate rejects MIXED signals below DQS 60. In choppy markets, most signals are MIXED, so the system sits idle for long periods.

Execution & Infrastructure

  • Slippage not measured — Market orders on Bybit will have slippage. The paper trade simulation uses the 15m close price, which may be significantly different from actual fill price. No slippage modeling exists.
  • Fee impact not analyzed — At 0.055% taker per side (0.11% round trip), fees on small moves eat most of the profit. The XRP trade made +$95 gross but only +$95 net after $28 in fees — on a $25k notional, that's 0.38% net on a 5% price move.
  • No position-level TP/SL on Bybit — TP/SL are attached as order parameters at placement. If the Bybit WS disconnects, the backend monitor can't trigger exits. Bybit's server-side TP/SL should handle this, but it hasn't been stress-tested.
  • No reconnection logic — If the pipeline container loses Bybit connectivity (as happened), it silently fails to place orders or reconcile. No alerting on missed trades.
  • Bybit leverage not set — The system assumes 4x leverage for sizing, but Bybit account leverage is set to 10x (visible in closed PnL data). Margin requirements may be lower than expected, but liquidation risk is higher.

Risk Management

  • No daily loss limit — If the system hits 5 SLs in a day, it keeps trading. No circuit breaker exists.
  • No drawdown protection — Paper account is down 7.4% with no automatic stop. A max drawdown threshold should halt trading.
  • Concentration risk — SOLUSDT has 17 of 54 closed trades (31%). The 25% SYMBOL_ALLOC_CAP was on notional, not trade count. No per-symbol trade frequency limit.
  • Funding rate not in PnL — Paper trade PnL doesn't account for funding payments/receipts. On Bybit, funding can be significant for positions held >8h.

Operations

  • No monitoring dashboard for pipeline health — If the pipeline stops running, there's no alert. The health endpoint on :8201 exists but isn't monitored.
  • Container zombie issue — Docker containers frequently can't be stopped due to zombie processes. Requires manual sudo kill -9 + docker rm -f. This is an infrastructure issue that will block smooth deployments.
  • No CI/CD pipeline — All changes are deployed manually via docker compose up -d --build. No automated testing before deploy.
  • Telegram bot untested for Bybit alerts — Notifications may only cover paper trades. Bybit-specific alerts (order placed, order failed, reconciliation discrepancy) may not be wired.

Recommendations Before Going Live

Do not go live on Bybit mainnet yet. The paper trading strategy is losing money. Fix the strategy first, then validate on demo, then go live.

  1. Audit SL placement — Analyze why 59% of trades hit SL. Compare SL distance to typical ATR range. Consider widening SL multiplier from 2-2.5x to 3-4x.
  2. Raise DQS minimum — Consider raising the execution threshold from DQS 50 to DQS 65+. The 50-64 band (Kelly 0.3x) is producing negative EV trades.
  3. Backtest vs live divergence — Run the backtester on the same period as live trading and compare signal-by-signal. Identify where the backtest was optimistic.
  4. Add daily loss limit — Halt trading if daily PnL < −2% of balance. Reset at UTC midnight.
  5. Measure slippage — Compare Bybit fill prices to the 15m close used by paper trades. If slippage > 0.1%, adjust strategy parameters.
  6. Set Bybit leverage to 4x — Currently 10x on the account. Mismatch with the system's 4x assumption could cause liquidation on larger positions.
  7. Fix container zombie issue — Investigate why Docker can't stop containers. Likely a kernel/apparmor issue with the snap Docker installation.
